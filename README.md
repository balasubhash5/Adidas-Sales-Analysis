# 👟 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, operating profit, units sold, and retailer contributions. It provides insights into product categories, gender-based sales quantities, sales methods, and geographical performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor total sales, operating profit, price per unit, and units sold.
- Compare operating profit across retailers.
- Analyze sales distribution by product category.
- Compare units sold across product categories and gender types.
- Evaluate sales performance across different sales methods.
- Identify high-performing cities and states.
- Explore geographical sales trends across regions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Data Visualization** – Cards, bar charts, column charts, pie charts, donut charts, and line charts

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators
Displays total sales, operating profit, price per unit, and total units sold.

### 2. Operating Profit by Retailer
Compares retailer contributions to operating profit and highlights differences in performance.

### 3. Sales by Product Category
Shows sales distribution across product categories to identify important contributors to revenue.

### 4. Units Sold by Category and Gender
Compares product quantities across categories and gender types to explore purchasing patterns.

### 5. Sales Method Analysis
Visualizes sales distribution by sales method to understand how different channels contribute to overall sales.

### 6. City-Wise Operating Profit
Compares operating profit across cities to identify stronger-performing locations.

### 7. State-Wise Sales Analysis
Displays sales performance by state to support geographical comparisons.

### 8. Regional Sales Analysis
Provides a regional view of sales performance to help evaluate differences across business areas.

## 💡 Business Benefits
- Helps monitor sales and profitability.
- Identifies high-performing retailers and product categories.
- Supports analysis of sales channels and customer purchasing patterns.
- Highlights geographical sales opportunities.
- Enables data-driven sales and business planning.
